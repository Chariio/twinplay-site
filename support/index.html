TwinPlay Support

Questions or problems? Email twinplay.support@gmail.com or reach out on X at @TwinPlayEmu.

We usually answer within 2 business days. English or Spanish.

Before you write

  • TwinPlay does not include or download games. You add game files that you own.
  • If a game does not run right, you can report it from inside the app. You can also email us with the game name, the system, your iPhone or iPad model, and the TwinPlay version (in the app’s settings).
  • Purchases are restored with Restore Purchases in the app. We never see your Apple ID or payment details.
